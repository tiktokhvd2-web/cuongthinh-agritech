## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural bridge between the wild, pristine agricultural heritage of the Northwest Highlands (Tây Bắc) and advanced, certified stainless-steel food processing technology. It is tailored for global B2B procurement officers, enterprise agricultural buyers, and industrial supply partners who demand certified quality (ISO 22000, HACCP, FDA), unyielding traceability, and institutional stability.

The design movement combines **Industrial Precision** with **Contemporary Minimalist Agri-Tech**. Rather than rustic artisanal tropes, the visual language relies on machined contours, crisp engineered borders, stainless steel (inox) visual cues, high-density data readouts, and warm, tactile canvas tones. The emotional response is one of rock-solid dependability, hygienic clarity, agricultural authenticity, and cutting-edge process control.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-tech processing in natural origin:
- **Primary — Deep Forest Green (`#205C3B`)**: Represents botanical vitality and deep alpine canopy. Used for core brand elements, primary CTAs, active operational indicators, and authoritative structure. Key scale variations: Darker `#17452C` (hover states, structural headers), Lighter `#2C7A50` (interactive accents, selected states).
- **Secondary — Harvest Gold (`#D97706`)**: Evokes agricultural yield, drying heat, testing validation, and high-value quality markers. Darker variant `#B45309` is reserved for certification badges, critical alert tags, and batch status highlights.
- **Tertiary — Inox Charcoal (`#4B5563`) & Machine Deep Slate (`#1F2937`, `#111827`)**: Embodies precision CNC machining, food-grade 304/316 stainless steel housings, and calibrated instrumentation. Used for high-legibility typographic hierarchies and crisp structural lines.
- **Neutral Canvas — Warm Highland Cream (`#F6F4E6`)**: A tactile, non-glare, organic background that softens heavy industrial data. Accompanied by Muted Cream (`#ECE8D4`) for card backdrops and container surfaces, eliminating sterile digital whites in favor of natural substrate warmth.

Color rules:
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is used sparingly, strictly for data card interiors to mimic sterile cleanroom surfaces against the warm cream canvas.
- Machine borders leverage `#1F2937` at 12% to 20% alpha to preserve technical edge definition without creating visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances calibrated mechanical metrics with dense transactional readability:
- **Headings & Metric Displays (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an authoritative, technical atmosphere with idiosyncratic geometric cuts reminiscent of precision instruments and factory signage. Numeric metrics, lot numbers, moisture percentages, and ISO classifications leverage Space Grotesk’s tabular qualities to reinforce technical governance.
- **Body & Data Specifications (`Inter`)**: Deployed across all descriptive paragraphs, export terms, lab analysis breakdowns, and table cells. It provides flawless multi-lingual rendering (supporting dense Vietnamese diacritics effortlessly) with tall x-height and neutral geometry.
- **Labels & Micro-Indicators (`Space Grotesk`)**: Always styled with generous tracking (0.04em to 0.08em) and uppercase transformation when identifying compliance statuses (e.g., `HACCP CERTIFIED`, `BATCH ID: TB-9042`).

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **12-column fluid grid** for desktop and a **4-column responsive grid** for mobile, driven by mathematical harmony and operational rigor:
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, outer section margin of 3rem (`48px`). Content spans in logical pairs or triplets (3-6-3 or 4-4-4) to support side-by-side spec sheets and product diagnostics.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 2rem (`32px`) margin.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, 0.75rem (`12px`) gutters, 1rem (`16px`) margin. Technical data tables switch to horizontally scrollable swipe containers with locked left-column identifiers.

Spacing Rhythm:
- Padding within components adheres strictly to base-4 intervals: `space-xs` (4px) for micro badge insets, `space-sm` (8px) for input and button vertical padding, `space-md` (16px) for compact card paddings, `space-lg` (24px) for structural container padding, and `space-xl` (40px) for layout separation between diagnostic groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows or skeuomorphic bevels. Instead, depth is articulated through **Machined Tonal Layering** and **Fine Technical Outlines**:
- **Base Canvas**: Surface neutral warm cream (`#F6F4E6`).
- **Mid-Tier (Sub-panels & Processing Bays)**: Muted cream container (`#ECE8D4`) bounded by a 1px structural machine line (`rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.12)`).
- **Upper-Tier (Interactive Modules & Lab Dossiers)**: Sterile Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) with a dual perimeter: a 1px solid stroke (`rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.08)`) accompanied by an ambient diffusion shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)`).
- **Focus / In-Inspection Elevation**: Elevates elements using a calibrated 1px inner bright inox highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) paired with a razor-thin outer boundary of Deep Forest Green (`#205C3B`).

## Shapes

The shape architecture is calibrated at **Level 1 (Soft)**, reflecting industrial sheet-metal radiusing—softened just enough to indicate precision milling without sliding into consumer-soft playfulness:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Tags)**: 0.25rem (`4px`) corner radius. This gives edge crispness, resembling physical equipment control plates.
- **Cards & Data Modules**: 0.5rem (`8px`) corner radius (`rounded-lg`), delivering structural containment.
- **Modals, Spec Sheets & Hero Panels**: 0.75rem (`12px`) corner radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Circular Indicators**: Full radius (`9999px`) is reserved solely for batch status pips (e.g., active drying indicator) and ISO/HACCP certified check badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Industrial Action)**: Deep Forest Green (`#205C3B`) background, Crisp White text in Space Grotesk, 0.25rem radius, 1px subtle dark border (`#17452C`). On hover: `#17452C` with a 1px accent hairline.
- **Secondary (Technical Inox)**: Muted Cream (`#ECE8D4`) fill, Charcoal text (`#1F2937`), framed with a distinct 1px border (`rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.2)`).
- **Tertiary / B2B Export Inquiries**: Harvest Gold (`#D97706`) background with white bold typography for quotation or bulk dispatch requests.

### Chips & Badges
- **Certification Badges**: Pill-framed or soft-cornered with Space Grotesk uppercase typography. E.g., `ISO 22000:2018` rendered with `#17452C` background and `#F6F4E6` text.
- **Metric Chips (Moisture, Brix, Mesh size)**: White background, 1px border (`#1F2937` at 15%), Charcoal mono-style text with green dot status.

### Lists & Data Grids
- Designed as industrial manifests: alternating subtle rows (`#F6F4E6` and `#ECE8D4`), separated by fine horizontal lines (`rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.08)`).
- Column headers set in Space Grotesk, 12px, uppercase, tracking +0.05em, paired with Inter 14px numeric/text data.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Square profile with 2px radius for checkboxes; circular with an engineered concentric inner ring for radio buttons.
- Inactive: 1.5px solid border in `#4B5563` against white canvas.
- Active: Filled with Deep Forest Green (`#205C3B`) containing a crisp white tick mark.

### Input Fields
- Background in sterile white (`#FFFFFF`) to visually separate data entry from the warm cream page canvas.
- Borders: 1px uniform stroke (`#1F2937` at 20%), 4px radius. 
- Focused state: 1.5px stroke in `#205C3B` with zero fuzzy glow—clean mechanical precision.
- Labels: Space Grotesk 12px Medium, floating or statically pinned above with mandatory asterisk in `#D97706`.

### Cards
- **Product & Processing Machinery Cards**: White surface, 1px border in `#1F2937` at 10% opacity, 8px radius. Split-panel design where visual photography (e.g., freeze-dried macadamia, Northwest star anise, processing line) is housed in the upper tier, while bottom tier houses technical data grids (origin altitude, drying standard, moisture threshold).
